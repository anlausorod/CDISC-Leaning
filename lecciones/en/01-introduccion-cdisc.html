Introduction to CDISC

CDISC (Clinical Data Interchange Standards Consortium) is a non-profit organization that develops standards to collect, organize, analyze and exchange clinical research data.

Regulatory agencies such as the FDA (United States) and the PMDA (Japan) require CDISC-formatted data in submissions.

Main standards

StandardWhat it is for
CDASHStandardizes data collection on CRFs (case report forms).
SDTMOrganizes the study's tabulation data into domains (DM, AE, LB, VS…).
ADaMDefines the analysis datasets (ADSL, BDS, OCCDS…).
SENDThe application of SDTM to nonclinical studies.
Define-XMLMetadata describing the submitted datasets.
CTControlled Terminology: lists of allowed values, maintained together with NCI EVS.

Typical data flow

  • CRF · CDASH
  • →
  • Tabulation data · SDTM
  • →
  • Analysis data · ADaM
  • →
  • Tables, listings and figures

All of it is documented with Define-XML.
